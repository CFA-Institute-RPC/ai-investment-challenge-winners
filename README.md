# CFA Institute AI Investment Challenge — Winning Solutions

Curated by the CFA Institute Research and Policy Center ([RPC Labs](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/themes/technology/rpclabs)).

## Background

The [CFA Institute AI Investment Challenge](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/events/ai-investment-challenge) is a student competition in which university teams apply artificial intelligence to real-world investment problems, building practical skills in investment analysis, data, and the responsible use of AI. Teams work through a structured, multi-stage format — concept submission, solution development (prototype and investment insight), and a final presentation to a panel of judges — with guidance from faculty advisors, industry professionals, and technology experts.

The inaugural challenge (2026) ran as a pilot with CFA Society United Kingdom. It fielded 28 teams; five finalists presented their solutions to the judges, and three teams took a place on the podium.

This repository indexes the three winning teams' solutions. Each entry links to the team's own public repository. The code remains owned and maintained by the teams; this page exists to provide attribution and a single place to discover their work. **Nothing here is a fork or copy of the teams' repositories.**
